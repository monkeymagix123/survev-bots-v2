# Goals of the bot system

Want an enjoyable experience playing against the bots
Want to be able to train with bots of varying difficulty
- train against humanlike bots, or
- more targeted bots for training

Want bots that simulate average human players, and bots that simulate pros for instance

Also bots that work well together in duos, faction, etc and can fight well as a group / swarm

They would be different types of bots
